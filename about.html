The Bitter Truth

Sustainability is for people who haven't seen our prices.

In 2019, Vaibhav was unceremoniously removed from EKATRA's artisan collective for proposing a line of single-use luxury items made from unrecyclable polymers. They called it irresponsible. He called it visionary. He left. He wept. He found plastic in his pocket and thought: this is it.

JEBKATRA was incorporated three weeks later in a Pune flat with one laptop, zero ethics certifications, and a deep personal commitment to making objects no one asked for.
