# JEBKATRA × EKATRA — Social Strategy & Script Bank

Season-based Instagram strategy, produced jointly by both teams. Companion to [README.md](README.md) (context + ideology) and [JEBKATRA-Launch-Scripts.pdf](JEBKATRA-Launch-Scripts.pdf) (shoot-ready scripts for Videos 1–2).

## The premise

A staged feud ("kayfabe") across two Instagram accounts. EKATRA = the composed, sustainable, quietly exasperated ex-employer. JEBKATRA (Vaibhav) = the unhinged rejected founder who monetised his firing. Both teams are in on it; the public never hears that. Every episode quietly ends at the JEBKATRA site.

### Non-negotiable rules
1. **Never break kayfabe** — in posts, comments, DMs. One slip kills the format.
2. **EKATRA is the straight man** — no bits, one-line corporate replies, never replies twice in a thread.
3. **Every episode sells something** — each reel maps to a product/page, except designated love-building episodes.
4. **Hinglish dialogue, English on-screen text.**
5. **Hook by second 1.5** — open mid-conflict.
6. **Comments are episodes** — scripted comment wars; best audience comments get pinned/reposted.
7. **Site sync before posting** — product exists, price correct, or the joke becomes a refund request.

## The Heated Argument scene — pointers & line banks

Both actors argue from a REAL moral position. Vaibhav must sound *almost right*; Aishwarya gets quieter as he gets louder. Each gets exactly one "almost-win" beat.

**Moral cores:** Vaibhav = *survival* ("main artisans ko amir banana chahta hoon, tum unhe sirf noble rakhna chahti ho"). Aishwarya = *dignity* ("hum cheezein nahi, bharosa bechte hain").

### Flow (4 beats + mask-slip)

**Beat 1 — trigger:**
- V: "Humara sabse accha bag 8 saal chalta hai. 8 SAAL. Customer wapas hi nahi aata. Hum apni hi success ke shikaar hain."
- A: "Woh wapas nahi aata kyunki usse zaroorat nahi hai. Isi ko imandaari kehte hain."
- V: "Isi ko bankruptcy bhi kehte hain."

**Beat 2 — his strongest ground (PICK ONE; the "Lata didi ka beta" device was tried and REJECTED — do not use named artisans' families):**
- *Scale:* "Cheez do mahine mein khatam ho jaaye toh 200 nahi, 2000 bikenge. Dus guna kaam, dus guna salary. Main artisans ke khilaf nahi — main unka PROMOTION hoon!" / A: "Tum unka kaam dus guna karke usko kachra bula rahe ho."
- *Market reality (strongest):* "Log 300 ka handmade nahi khareedte, 3000 ka plastic wala khareedte hain — kyunki uspe logo hai. Main bas duniya ke hisaab se chal raha hoon." / A: "Duniya ke hisaab se toh paani bhi neeche behta hai. Company isliye banayi thi ki hum upar chalein."
- *Self-pity:* "Paanch saal maine is jagah ko diye. Ek baar profit ki baat ki toh main villain?" / A: "Paanch saal mein bhi tum samjhe nahi ki hum yahan kya bana rahe hain."

**Beat 2.5 — THE MASK SLIP (the appraisal greed — this is what justifies the firing):**
- V: "Aur yeh har saal appraisal ka natak kya hai? Kaam wahi, silaai wahi — salary har saal upar kyun? HUMARA kya fayda?"
- V: "Loyalty ka paisa nahi hota. Woh yahan isliye hain kyunki unke paas option nahi hai. Aur jiske paas option nahi hai, usse raise kyun dena?"
- V: "Ek machine kharido — woh kabhi appraisal nahi maangti. Kabhi rota nahi."
- A (the trap closes): "Ek minute. Abhi tum salary double karne waale the. Ab appraisal bhi mat do. …Dono mein se jhooth kaunsa tha, Vaibhav?"
- V (caught): "Woh… pitch tha." / A: "Haan. Wahi toh problem hai. Tumhare liye sab pitch hai."

**Beat 3 — she turns it personal:**
- A: "Yeh aurtein pehli baar apne naam se kama rahi hain. Jab woh apna banaya bag kisi ke haath mein dekhti hain — woh feeling tum spreadsheet mein kahan likhoge?"
- V: "Feelings ka invoice nahi banta, Aish!"
- A (quiet, deadly): "Tumne bhi yeh feeling mehsoos ki thi. Yaad hai? Ab tumhe sirf margin yaad hai. Tum badal gaye ho."

**Beat 4 — the unforgivable line:**
- V (loud): "Haan badal gaya hoon! Kyunki maine samajh liya — log cheez ko value TABHI karte hain jab woh khatam ho jaati hai! Cheez ko amar banaogi, toh khud mar jaogi. Single. Use. Luxury. Yehi future hai!"
- A (long pause, calm): "…Nikal jao. Aur jaate waqt kuch bhi handmade mat chhoona."

**Direction:** volume see-saw (her whisper beats his shout) · his almost-win = market-reality line · hers = "tum badal gaye ho" · end on 2s of silent eye contact — that's the thumbnail.

- **"Lata didi ka beta" device REJECTED** — too personal; never build emotional beats on a named artisan's family. Use Scale / Market / Self-pity angles instead.
